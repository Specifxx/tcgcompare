The wordmarks of lor, gun, ua, dg, swu, fab, ws, cfv, sor, ga, gz, cp, sve, uvs, ele, cr, pal, wx, bss, ac are our own plain text (Inter Black, letter-spaced, in each game's colour), drawn 2026-11-17. No public-domain logo exists for them and their owners publish no licence for third-party price sites; swap one for the official logo only where a press kit or written licence allows it. Riot's press page (riotgames.com/en/press) lists no Riftbound logo and its fan policy bars logo use without a written licence (media@riotgames.com).
