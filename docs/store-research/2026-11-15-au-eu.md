# Own-store candidates, AU and EU (2026-11-15)

Found by four parallel web searches. Every shop below answered `/meta.json` as a Shopify store charging its market's
currency (AUD or EUR) and is on no sister site, in no earlier research file and not banned. Where the games column is
empty, its collections were not read: the weekly crawl probe reads every shop's `/collections.json` and keeps only
collections that name one game, so a shop with none simply drops out. Legitimacy was checked lightly (own domain,
real address or store); `lib/import/vet.ts` still judges every store on every import.

Left out: Funporium (sports cards only), Gamesco and Western Front Games (no sealed TCG), KartenKoloss (Korean and
Chinese product), and shops already listed. NZ shops are out: TCG Prices has no NZ market.
